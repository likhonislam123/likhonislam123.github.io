About Me

আমার সম্পর্কে — a little about who I am and what I'm building.

I'm Md Soaib Islam, known to everyone as Likhon. I make videos with artificial intelligence — stories, characters and worlds that exist only in imagination, brought to life on screen.

My best-known creation is Sheru, a white lion in a white suit with a gold chain, whose adventures — family moments, TV breaking news, and big story arcs — are the heart of my channel. I also make funny videos and cartoon songs for kids, the kind children love to watch again and again.

Beyond videos, I've built a car driving game — drive through traffic, dodge the other cars, and reach the finish line to win. My goal is simple: grow Likhon AI World on YouTube and Facebook, and earn by entertaining people.
